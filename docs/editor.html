How to play it

  • Drag a slider or knob. Wheel to nudge, Shift for fine steps, double-click to reset.
  • Two tablets, Lower and Upper, send a section to either half of the keyboard. The yellow line is the split point.
  • The amber display shows the preset, and the value of whatever you touched last.
  • Equalizer and Reverb keep their settings when you change preset.
